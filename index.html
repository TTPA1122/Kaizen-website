Translation & Transcreation

🔍

QA Testing

How We Work

From Brief to Build, Seamlessly

A proven four-stage pipeline that keeps your project on time and on quality.

01

Discovery

We audit your game files, define scope, identify target locales, and align on style guides and glossaries.

02

Localization

Native translators work in context. Voice actors record. Subtitles are timed. UI is adapted — all in parallel.

03

QA & Testing

Linguistic review, functional testing on target devices, cultural validation, and final polish.

04

Delivery

Final assets delivered in your preferred format. Post-launch support and iterative updates available.
